## ✨ Features

- **Royal Palace Editor Studio**:
  - Live side-by-side interactive preview with zero-flicker real-time synchronization.
  - 5 Luxury Color Palette Themes: *Emerald & Gold*, *Wine & Rose Gold*, *Midnight & Starlight*, *Pastel Clouds*, and *Terracotta & Marigold*.
  - Opening Experience options: *🚪 3D Royal Vault Opening Doors* and *📞 Incoming Royal Wedding Call Screen*.
  - Full couple and parental blessing customization.
  - Multi-ceremony timeline manager with dress codes, venue details, and Google Maps directions.
- **3D Animated Guest Experience**:
  - Embossed botanical double vault doors with gold beveled trims and glowing seam lights.
  - Ornate scalloped "TAP TO OPEN" tag with gold rope tassel and touch hints.
  - Snappy, fluid door opening animation (< 1s) with harmonic chimes, canvas confetti, and romantic soundtrack.
  - Classical marble courtyard fountain with animated water spritz and flowers under royal pleated silk drapes.
  - Arched "Save The Date" with one-click `.ics` Calendar export and Google Calendar link.
  - Curated wedding memories photo carousel.
  - Sacred Ceremonies timeline (Sangeet, Baraat & Varmala, Sacred Pheras, Governors Reception).
  - Interactive "Written with Love" feather quill signature animation.
  - RSVP confirmation with guest counter that confirms directly back via WhatsApp.
- **Direct WhatsApp & Email Dispatch**:
  - Form encodes customized wedding data into a shareable link.
  - 1-click WhatsApp dispatch directly opens chat targeting the guest's phone number.
  - 1-click Email dispatch with pre-filled invitation text.
- **Fully Responsive**:
  - Adaptive luxury experience on laptops, tablets, and mobile phones with zero manual viewport switches.

## 🚀 How to Enable GitHub Pages

1. Go to repository **Settings** on GitHub: `https://github.com/urvashizoopcoder-hub/ElegantWeddingTemplate/settings/pages`
2. Under **Build and deployment** > **Branch**, select `main` branch and `/ (root)` folder.
3. Click **Save**.
4. Within 1–2 minutes, your website will be live at:
   **https://urvashizoopcoder-hub.github.io/ElegantWeddingTemplate/**
